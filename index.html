Scientific Context

RVM geometry, polarization, and large-survey scale

The Rotating Vector Model (RVM) provides a geometrical framework for studying pulsar polarization, linking the observed variation of the linear polarization position angle across pulse phase to the geometrical relationship between the pulsar magnetic axis and the observer line of sight.

With the rapid development of large radio telescopes such as FAST and MeerKAT, together with high-sensitivity observation programs including GPPS and the MeerKAT Thousand Pulsar Array, the number of pulsars with polarization measurements has increased significantly, enabling systematic RVM-fitting studies on an unprecedented scale.
